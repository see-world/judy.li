# What Is Knowledge Governance?

Knowledge Governance provides the structures, responsibilities, processes, and controls that ensure knowledge remains accurate, trusted, accessible, and useful.

Knowledge Management focuses on creating and sharing knowledge.

Knowledge Governance focuses on stewardship, accountability, ownership, and quality.

As organizations adopt AI technologies, governance becomes increasingly important because AI relies on the quality of the knowledge it consumes.
